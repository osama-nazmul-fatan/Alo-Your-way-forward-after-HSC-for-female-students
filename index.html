From a new skill to your first income

Most beginners need a few months before their first paying client. This is the route that works.

  1. Learn

    Pick a free or low-cost course that fits your device and time.

  2. Practise

    Make 5 to 10 sample pieces you can show people.

  3. Earn locally

    Start with shops, Facebook pages and people you know.

  4. Grow

    Move to freelancing sites or a full-time job once you have reviews.

Before you start freelancing

  • Fiverr and Upwork accept members aged 18 and over.
  • You need your own NID and a bank or mobile wallet account to receive payments.
  • Payoneer or a bank transfer is the usual way to receive money from abroad.

Warning signs of a scam

  • A course that promises a fixed dollar income.
  • Anyone asking for money to give you a job.
  • Programs where you earn by bringing in other people.

For parents and guardians

Continuing to study does not have to cost a lot or mean moving far away. Many programs on Alo are free, pay a stipend, or run in your own district.

A daughter who finishes a degree, diploma or certified course can earn and support the family for years to come.
